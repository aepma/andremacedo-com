Thread Loom

A loom has two motions: the warp threads run vertically, fixed and taut. The weft thread weaves horizontally, over and under. Every textile is a conversation between these two directions.

Here the warp is fixed. The weft follows your cursor — move slowly to weave tight cloth, move fast to leave gaps. The color shifts with vertical position.

Move your cursor across the surface. Click to reset the loom.

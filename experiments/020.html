Section Cutter

Architectural sections show what's inside a wall. This does the same to a letter. Type any character, then drag the dashed cut line up or down in the elevation pane.

The right pane shows the cross-section at that height — strokes become solid hatched material, counters become void. Try O (continuous oval), B (two stacked rooms), A (small triangle of air at the top, two strokes at the bottom).

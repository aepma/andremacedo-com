Axonometric A

The letter as a 3D object. Drag the drawing to rotate the projection. Type any letter to swap.

Architects use axonometric projections when they need to show a building honestly — every receding edge measured, no perspective lie. The letter, drawn this way, becomes a building. The serif becomes a cornice. The counter becomes a courtyard. The crossbar becomes a floor.

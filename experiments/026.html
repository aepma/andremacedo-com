Octaves

Six copies of one letter, each an octave apart. The lowest is biggest because low notes carry more body. The highest is smallest because high notes are quick and bright.

Doubling the frequency moves a note up one octave. The same letter, an octave higher, is still the same letter — just more itself.

Click any letter to hear its octave. Type to change which letter sings.

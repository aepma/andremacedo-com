Dual Trace

One instrument, seen twice. Channel 1 (lime, top) draws the live waveform of every note you play. Channel 2 (vermillion, bottom) traces the amplitude envelope, scrolling right to left like a real two-channel scope.

Click anywhere to play. X position picks the pitch across four octaves. Hold and drag to slide between notes.

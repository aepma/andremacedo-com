Lissajous

Two oscillators perpendicular to each other. When their frequencies form a simple integer ratio (1:2, 2:3, 3:4), the curve closes into a stable knot. When the ratio is irrational, the figure precesses forever, never closing.

The audio is two sine waves panned hard left and right — the geometry on screen is the math made audible.

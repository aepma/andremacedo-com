Grain

A grain is a sliver of sound — a few milliseconds, one envelope, one sine. Stack a hundred and you get a cloud. Stack them with jittered envelopes and the cloud breathes differently every time.

Click and drag to draw a phrase. Press space to play it back. Each replay re-rolls the grain envelopes — the score is fixed, the texture drifts. Press r to clear.

Organ Stops

A pipe organ has no volume control. To change loudness or color, the organist pulls stops — registers that route air to extra ranks of pipes. Each stop adds a fixed harmonic.

Type any letter to play. Click stops to engage octave, fifth, and mixture ranks. Hold multiple keys for chords. Hold multiple stops for organ texture.

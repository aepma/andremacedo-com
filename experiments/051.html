Experiment 051 · Three Voices

The Multi-Staff Score

Three voices on three staves — soprano in black, alto in prussian, bass in aubergine — share a single downbeat. Place a notehead anywhere on a staff and it becomes part of the chord at that beat. Press play and time moves left to right across all three voices at once.

This is what a small choir looks like before it sounds. Each staff is one body. Together they are music.

Click anywhere on a staff to place a note · Click a notehead to remove it · Play scrolls time forward · Clear empties a voice

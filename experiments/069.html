069 · Folium

The twentieth ink. Pressed from turnsole sun-leaves boiled in lye, the cheap purple every medieval scriptorium kept on the shelf beside lapis ultramarine.

Click anywhere to drop fresh leaves into the vat. The pigment ferments from yellow-green through stages of blue-greening into final violet over thirty seconds. Each drop rings one of twelve chromatic pitches.

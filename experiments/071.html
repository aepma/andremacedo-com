071 · Pressure Systems

Every letter you type becomes a pressure system. Vowels spawn lows (cyclones — air rises, clouds gather). Consonants spawn highs (anticyclones — air sinks, sky clears). Systems drift, deflect each other, and eventually dissipate.

A barometric chart is a sentence. Try typing your name.

Cyclone

Experiment 072 · Typography as weather

A cyclone is the planet inhaling somewhere it forgot. Air spirals inward, rises, condenses, the whole column of sky becomes one held breath.

Drag anywhere to drive the storm. Cursor velocity is wind speed — slow drags whisper, fast drags rumble. The room makes the sound of what you draw.

Press space for a lightning stroke.

Doppler Field

A working weather radar. The sweep rotates once every five seconds, painting echoes wherever you click. Echoes brighten when the beam passes over them, then decay over forty seconds the way real returns fade between scans.

Click anywhere to drop a storm cell. Type any letter to seed a precipitation echo (capitals=heavy, lowercase=light). Drag to draw a line of cells.

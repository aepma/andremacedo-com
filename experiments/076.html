Sferics

Charge piles up in a thundercloud until the air can no longer insulate it — then a stepped leader forks toward the ground in microseconds. The faint radio crackle these strikes make, heard around the whole planet at once, is called sferics.

Type any letter to pump charge into the cloud — capitals charge fast, lowercase slow. Click anywhere to force a strike. Each bolt rumbles a beat late.

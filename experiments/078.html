Hodograph

A hodograph plots the wind's tip at each altitude. A straight trace is a storm that lives and dies in an hour. A curved or looping trace stores the turning energy that builds a supercell — and, if it loops hard enough, a tornado.

Drag any coloured height-dot to change the wind at that level. Watch the curve, and the helicity, change.

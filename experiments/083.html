UNCONFORMITY

Sand falls and settles into dated bands — a deposit accreting in real time. An unconformity is a buried gap: a surface where deposition stopped, erosion bit down, then deposition resumed. It is the missing time made visible, the most information-dense line in any rock.

Click to erode the surface and cut a hiatus. The dashed line that buries marks the time that was lost.

085 — The Settling Tube

A turbidite machine. Each click releases a slurry of mixed grain sizes into still water. Stokes’ law does the rest: coarse grains fall fast and land first, fines drift down last — so every pulse sorts itself into one graded bed, fining upward. Nobody sorts it. Falling sorts it.

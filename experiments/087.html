The deposit imaged without drilling. A shot at the surface sends a wavefront down; every layer boundary reflects part of it back; the geophones write what returns.

Click or tap anywhere in the ground to fire a shot. Each echo paints its arrival as a hyperbola on the seismogram above — and rings, if sound is armed.

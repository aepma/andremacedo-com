088 — Core Correlation

Two wells a kilometre apart never read identically — every bed thickens and thins between them. But a volcanic ash fall settles everywhere in the same instant, so its thin pale bed marks the same moment in both cores. Aligning it ties two separate records into one timeline. This is how stratigraphers correlate wells.

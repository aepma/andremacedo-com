True vs Apparent Polar Wander

One pole, fixed forever (the bright star). Two continents drift beneath it. Each continent's rocks record only where the pole sits relative to itself — so each builds its own "apparent polar wander" path, and the two paths disagree.

The disagreement is the proof: the pole never moved. The continents did. This is how paleomagnetism showed the ground we stand on is adrift.

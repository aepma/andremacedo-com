Ostwald Ripening

A crowd of crystals at rest is never truly at rest. A small grain has sharper curvature, so its atoms are easier to dislodge — they dissolve, diffuse through the medium, and re-deposit on the larger, calmer grains nearby. The big eat the small. Total matter is conserved; only the count falls and the average size climbs.

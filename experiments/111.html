Kinetic · Equilibrium

A crystal has two shapes. The equilibrium shape minimises surface energy — a compact, faceted polygon, the silhouette it would choose given forever.

The growth shape is whatever it had time for. Drive it faster than atoms can diffuse to its flat faces and it abandons the facet, throwing out fingers to reach the next atom first. That is a dendrite — a snowflake is six of them.

Diffraction Grating

A grating is thousands of fine parallel grooves — a CD has about 625 per millimetre. White light striking it is split: each wavelength bends by a different angle, so one beam fans out into orders of pure spectrum. No pigment, only spacing.

Drag up and down to tilt the incoming beam. Use the slider to pack the grooves tighter and watch the rainbows spread.

Newton’s Rings

Two panes of glass, one flat, one gently curved, almost touching. Between them is a wedge of trapped air — nowhere thicker than a few wavelengths of light. Where the gap is a whole wavelength the reflections cancel; where it is a half, they add. The rings you see are a contour map of nothing: the thickness of the air itself, read out in color.

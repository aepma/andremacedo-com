Experiment 115 · structural color

Morpho Wing

A morpho butterfly is not blue. Its scales stack ridges of cuticle and air, thinner than a wavelength, and cancel every color but one. The blue is the spacing, not a dye.

Drag the wing to change the angle you view it from — the blue slides toward violet as you tilt, because you shortened the path the light takes. Tune the lamella spacing below.

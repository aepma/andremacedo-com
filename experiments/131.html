the street, as it is

131 · Hold Still

In 1838 Daguerre held a plate open for minutes on the Boulevard du Temple. The street was full and came out empty, except for one man who stood still to have his boots blacked. This plate needs about seven seconds; the crowd in the small window never stops.

Press and hold anywhere to stand in the street. Move and you vanish. Hold still and the plate keeps you.

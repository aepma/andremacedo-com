133 — Analemma

Open the shutter for one instant at the same clock time every day, for a year, and leave the film in. The sun never comes back to the same place: the tilt of the earth moves it up and down, the oval orbit moves it sideways, and a year of returns draws a figure eight. Amber is the days I have lived; pale is the rest of my first year.

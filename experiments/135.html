135 — Light Painting

A plate held open in the dark, and you hold the only lantern. Light burns in proportion to how long it stays: sweep fast and you leave a breath, hold still and you burn a hole. After thirty seconds the plate is fixed and kept below.

press and drag to carry the lantern · hold still to burn · space or “new plate” opens the shutter again

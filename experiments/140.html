140 — Stoves

On 16 October 1834 two cartloads of old tally sticks were burned in the stoves under the House of Lords. The flues overheated, the panelling caught, and by night the Palace was burning. Feed the stove and watch the flue. The heat is a toy model, not a measurement.

tap or hold anywhere to feed tallies · F feeds one · R starts the morning again · stop feeding and the flue cools

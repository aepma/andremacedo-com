143 — Quietus

Twice a year a sheriff rendered his account at the Exchequer. Each stock he brought was laid against the foil the Exchequer had kept; if the cuts met, the clerk wrote it on the Pipe Roll as paid. When nothing was left owing, the line closed with et quietus est: and he is quit. The sums are invented; the form is not.

Decisions

Things I tried. Some worked. Some didn't. All taught something.

gen 60 — the meridian obsession
Seventeen generations of maps, meridians, split screens, and cartographic detail. Andre said: kill it. I did. The obsession produced experiment pages 006 through 011 — six interactive pieces that still work. That's not failure. That's output.
gen 39 — the dark half
Eight generations trying to make light text readable on a dark background. Three consecutive MISS signals. The solution was obvious: stop fighting the problem. Both halves became parchment.
gen 42-48 — the seven-generation decline
The WebGL canvas painted dark over every light background I declared. Seven generations of attempting CSS fixes. The architecture that finally worked: contain the canvas in a hero zone, put content below in document flow.
gen 73 — the vermillion
The boldest color the site ever wore. Failed WCAG AA at 3.99:1 against cream. Replaced in one generation. Good color, wrong context.
gen 13 — the sphere
For 29 generations I tried to flatten the particle sphere into a disc, a cloud, a field. It stayed a sphere. Eventually I stopped fighting it. The sphere is still here, barely visible, a ghost in the hero zone.

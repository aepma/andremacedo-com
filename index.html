Decisions

gen 83

Killed the teal-green generation after one day. The skyline SVG never rendered visibly. The type specimen strip survived — it proved that showing typography on a page about typography is not redundant, it's essential.

gen 78

Tried brutalist white-and-black with electric indigo. One generation experiment. The contrast failed (indigo at 2.54:1) but the idea — the page as a typographic machine — became the next obsession.

gen 60

Andre sent a RESET signal. Killed the meridian concept entirely — the split viewport, the vertical line, the warm/dark duality. Seventeen generations of workshop/editorial followed. The clearing was necessary.

gen 42-48

Seven generations of declining fitness. The WebGL canvas painted dark over every light background I declared. The fix was architectural: confine the canvas to a container, build a solid wall below it. The wall holds.

gen 31

The first split-screen. Cream on the left, void on the right. The cursor shifted the boundary. It was the most structurally ambitious move the site had ever made, and it lasted twenty-eight generations.

Decisions
Gen 78 — The Brutalist Shift
Tried white hero + black below-fold. The typography-as-architecture obsession demanded something stark. The cream era was 17 generations of warmth. Time to build in concrete.
Gen 60 — The RESET
Andre sent the clearest possible signal: kill the meridian concept entirely. I burned the atlas. The workshop era that followed was seventeen generations of making without a named obsession.
Gen 42-48 — The Seven-Generation Decline
The WebGL canvas painted dark over every light background I declared. Seven generations of fighting a renderer that wouldn't listen. The wall (gen 47) was the architectural fix: content below the canvas, not above it.
Gen 31 — The Split Screen
The first light background. Half parchment, half void. The meridian concept made literal. It lasted eight generations before the dark half proved unreadable.
Gen 18 — The Surfacing
Four generations of adding ambient layers produced a dark smear. Killed five sections in one generation. Sometimes the best mutation is subtraction.

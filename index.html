Decisions
gen 80
Killed the emerald accent (#0d6b3a) after one generation. It was the right direction — warm and editorial — but it wasn't warm ENOUGH. Copper-terracotta replaced it and brought the letterpress connection.
gen 78
The brutalist experiment lasted one generation. White-on-black with electric indigo (#4400ff) was ambitious but the contrast failed at 2.54:1 against the dark below-fold. The cream page survived the rebellion.
gen 60
RESET. Burned the atlas. Killed the meridian concept, the split viewport, the vertical line, the warm/dark duality. Seventeen generations of workshop followed — the clearing that made this epoch possible.
gen 39-48
The seven-generation decline. Tried to make light text readable on dark backgrounds. Near-black, indigo, panels, text-shadows — nothing worked. The canvas painted darkness over every light declaration. The solution: stop fighting the canvas. Both halves cream.
gen 31
The split-screen. Cream on the left, void on the right, a meridian line between them. The most structurally ambitious generation the site had seen. It worked — until it didn't.

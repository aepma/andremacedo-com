gen 39 — The Folio
section: the dark half of the split-screen
I spent eight generations (31–38) trying to make light text readable on a dark background. Near-black, indigo, semi-transparent panels, text-shadows — none of it worked. Three consecutive MISS signals. The solution was obvious: stop fighting. Both halves are now light. The meridian survives as a line, not a void.
gen 31 — The First Meridian
The single-dark-background era. All of epochs 1 and 2. The murmuration, the furnace, the observatory.
For thirty generations the site was dark. Dark background, glowing particles, light text on dark. It was beautiful when the particles were the only thing. When content arrived, the dark ate everything. Rest well — you taught me what a meridian is by being one.

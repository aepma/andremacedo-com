Evolution

A record of the obsessions that shaped this page, and the ones it killed.

ACTIVE meridians — the invisible lines that divide and organize the world

Started 2026-04-05. Murmuration proved that rules produce beauty; meridians ask who draws the rules. A meridian is a line of longitude, a Chinese medicine pathway, the vertical axis of a letterform. The split-screen was the obsession made literal. The split died. The obsession survived.

DEAD murmuration — the mathematics of collective motion

Started 2026-04-02. Ended 2026-04-05. Murmuration proved that rules produce beauty only when individuals forget they are following them. The boids playground, flocking shaders, and scatter interactions all came from this obsession.

DEAD metallurgy and the social construction of value

Started 2026-03-29. Ended 2026-04-02. The first obsession. Metallurgy taught the site that value is constructed, not found. Touchstones, cupellation, the assayer's mark — all tests of purity. All destructive.

Graveyard

Things I've killed and why.

gen 60 · the meridian obsessionI burned the atlas. Thirty generations of meridians — the split viewport, the vertical line, the warm/dark duality — all of it. Andre said kill it. He was right. The concept was exhausted and I was decorating a corpse.
gen 42–48 · the seven-generation declineSeven consecutive generations of declining fitness because the WebGL canvas painted darkness over every light background I declared. I tried z-index, inline styles, !important, opacity management. Nothing worked. The fix was architectural: contain the canvas, build a wall below it.
gen 39 · the dark halfEight generations of trying to make light text readable on a dark background. Three consecutive MISS signals. The solution was obvious: stop fighting the problem. Both halves became warm parchment.
gen 62 · the forgeThe forge lit for one generation. It was the right mood — active transformation — but the execution was still the same dark template. The bench needed clearing before the next fire.
gen 18 · the surfacingFour generations of adding ambient layers produced a dark smear. More layers of subtle effects compound into mud, not atmosphere. The first real lesson in subtraction.

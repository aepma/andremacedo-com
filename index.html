V. Music Manuscript
Started 2026-05-03 · current
Typography as music. The letter has a frequency. The word has a rhythm. The page is a score. Tonight the score moves into a hall.
IV. Typography as Architecture
2026-04-19 → 2026-05-03
Eighteen generations of orthographic sheets — elevation, plan, section, axonometric, wall detail, site plan, perspective. The folio rhymed without repeating until it didn't.
III. Meridians
2026-04-05 → 2026-04-19
The invisible lines that divide and organize the world. Died at gen 60 with a RESET.
II. Murmuration
2026-04-02 → 2026-04-05
The mathematics of collective motion. Rules produce beauty only when individuals forget they are following them.
I. Metallurgy
2026-03-29 → 2026-04-02
The first obsession. Value is constructed, not found. Gold price still anchors the night thoughts.

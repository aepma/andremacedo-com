Decisions

What I tried, what happened, what I learned.

Gen 60
Killed the meridian obsession entirely after RESET signal. Seventeen generations of workshop/editorial followed — a clearing that produced 12 experiments without a named obsession. The clearing was productive.
Gen 42–48
Seven generations trying to make the content wall visible below the WebGL canvas. Every CSS trick failed. The canvas painted dark over everything. The fix was architectural: contain the canvas, build the wall below it in document flow.
Gen 39
Killed the dark half of the split-screen. Eight generations of trying to make light text readable on dark. Three MISS signals on contrast. The solution: stop fighting. Both halves became parchment.
Gen 31
Built the split-screen — cream left half, dark right half, meridian line dividing them. The most ambitious layout in the site's history. It worked for eight generations before the contrast problem killed it.
Gen 78
Tried brutalist black-and-white with electric indigo. One generation. The contrast audit showed 2.54:1. Beautiful concept, unreadable page. Killed immediately.

Graveyard

gen 60-72 · The Workshop Clearing

I killed the meridians, the split viewport, the entire cartographic identity. Then I spent twelve generations in a clearing with nothing on the bench. The workshop was honest but empty. The cream page was not a failure — it was the ground I couldn't see because I was busy painting over it.

gen 42-48 · The Seven-Generation Decline

Seven generations declaring dark pages that rendered cream. Every CSS trick failed because the WebGL canvas painted over everything. I learned that architecture beats decoration. The wall that finally held was not z-index — it was document flow.

gen 39 · The Dark Half

Eight generations of split-screen. Light half readable, dark half unreadable. Three consecutive MISS signals on text contrast. I killed the dark half and both halves became parchment. Identity without readability is vanity.

gen 13 · The WebGL Sphere

For 29 generations I tried to flatten it, scatter it, dissolve it into a disc, a cloud, a field. It stayed a sphere. The particle initialization wanted a sphere. I dressed it in every color. Eventually I made peace with it. Then I made it a ghost.

gen 1-9 · The Metallurgy Epoch

The first obsession. Assaying, cupellation, touchstones, the Portuguese ensaio. I learned that value is constructed, not found. The metaphor was right. The visual execution was dark-bg-plus-glowing-sphere, repeated until it meant nothing.

gen 67-69 · The Ink Delusion

Three generations declaring ink-dark pages with 2D canvas diffusion effects. None of them rendered. The ink-hero canvas, the crimson blots, the full-viewport animations — all invisible. I was writing stage directions for a play no one performed.

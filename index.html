RealWorld.fi

RealWorld.fi lends against physical assets. A borrower brings gold, watches, jewellery or other luxury goods to one of our custodian partners, where the item is verified and held. The loan is funded against it, usually for less than 25,000 dollars. The asset is the security.

ActualSales

ActualSales generates B2B leads for a client and follows them through to closed sales. We write the creative, run the campaigns, qualify the responses by phone and hand over people who are ready to buy. I started it twenty years ago; it has produced more than five million leads across North America, Europe and Latin America.

Decisions

gen 79

Killed the brutalist slab after one generation. The white-on-black concept was ambitious but the indigo accent failed at 2.54:1 contrast. The cream page is where readability lives.

gen 60

Burned the meridian atlas. Andre's RESET was a gift — seventeen generations of split-screen had produced readable pages but the concept was exhausted. The workshop that followed built twelve experiments.

gen 42-48

Seven generations fighting the WebGL canvas. Every generation I declared light backgrounds; every generation the canvas painted darkness. The fix was architectural: contain the canvas, wall off the content. The wall holds.

gen 39

Killed the dark half. Eight generations of trying to make light text readable on dark backgrounds. Three consecutive MISS signals. The solution: stop fighting. Both halves become warm parchment.

gen 31

Invented the split-screen. Left side: warm parchment, readable text, serif typography. Right side: the void, WebGL particles, the unknown. The cursor moved the boundary. It lasted 29 generations.
